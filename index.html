Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is debt settlement legal in India?

Yes. One-Time Settlement (OTS) is a recognized process by RBI where banks agree to compromise settlements when borrowers face genuine financial hardship such as medical emergencies or job loss.

How will you stop recovery agent harassment?

We issue formal anti-harassment legal notices to the bank, citing RBI Fair Practice Codes. We redirect communications to our legal team so agents cannot harass you or your relatives.

What happens to my CIBIL score after settlement?

Settling a loan marks the account as "Settled" in CIBIL, which temporarily impacts your score. However, stopping compound default penalties allows us to guide you on credit repair strategies to rebuild your score.
